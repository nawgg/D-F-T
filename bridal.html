How a Bridal Fitting Works

  1. Come in for a fitting — no appointment needed, just bring your dress, wedding shoes and the undergarments you will wear.
  2. One fitting — we pin, measure and plan every change together with you. One fitting is enough.
  3. Final pickup — pressed, finished and ready for your wedding day.

When to start: we recommend starting 1-3 weeks before your wedding where possible. Last-minute? Ask us — we will do our best.
